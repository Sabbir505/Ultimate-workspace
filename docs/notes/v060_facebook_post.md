## Main post (video attached)

Relay 0.6 is out — and this is the release where your workspace starts working
without you.

→ Vault. A folder of plain markdown on your own disk that your agent can actually
work in — read it, write to it, move notes around, file what it learns. Tag search,
wikilinks, a local graph, an in-app PDF viewer. Your knowledge stays yours.

→ Local image generation. Describe an image, get a PNG — painted by a model running
on your machine. No cloud, no API key, nothing leaving the computer.

→ Automations that fire without you. On a schedule, or from a webhook, a file
change, a new commit, or new email. They run even while Relay is closed.

→ Your rules, your guardrails. Hooks run your own scripts before and after every
agent tool call — approve, deny, or rewrite what the agent is doing.

Same promise as day one: your agents, your models, your machine.

## Posting notes

- Upload the film natively to Facebook (not a YouTube link) — native video gets
  materially better reach than an outbound link.
- Use the 9:16 cut for Feed and Reels; keep the 16:9 master for the desktop/watch
  post if you split them.
- The first ~125 characters are what shows before "See more" — keep the hook line
  exactly as written.
- If reach flags, move the download link to the first comment ("link in the
  comments") — Facebook slightly demotes posts with external links in the body.
- Hashtags: 2–3 maximum; Facebook ignores the rest. #Relay #LocalFirst #AIagents
  are the kept set.
